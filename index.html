😡 The Customers

😊 Nice customers – They make your day.

😐 Confused customers – They ask where the power button is... while the device is ON.

😡 Angry customers – Somehow they think you personally caused every problem.

Remember: They're angry at the company... you're just the unlucky person who answered the call.

🎭 Your New Vocabulary

"Please stay on the line."

Translation: "I'm secretly asking Google and my team leader for help."

Enjoy it while it lasts.
